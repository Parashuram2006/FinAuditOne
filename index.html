The 6-Bucket Matching Engine

Statutory Accuracy for Tax Audits

Cross-examines client Daybook purchase registers against official GSTR-2B returns with full Credit Note and Amendment handling.

1. Exact & Fuzzy Matchers

Matches identical invoices within custom tolerance (≤ ₹5). Automatically cleans punctuation, financial-year suffixes, and leading zeroes to resolve format drift (e.g. `INV/26/04` vs `4`).

2. At-Risk ITC & AP Freeze

Flags purchase vouchers in the client's books that suppliers omitted from GSTR-1. Directly enforces Section 16(2)(aa) AP payment holds to protect clients from mandatory Rule 37A reversals.

3. Unclaimed ITC Recovery

Catches genuine invoices declared by suppliers in GSTR-2B that accountants forgot to record in the Daybook, preventing tax credit forfeiture before the November 30 statutory deadline.

4. Credit Note (CDNR) Reversals

Seamlessly incorporates Section 34 Credit Notes without double-subtraction errors, ensuring net compliant credit claims precisely match government assessment records.

5. Tax Discrepancy Isolation

Identifies invoices where vendors declared different GST rates or amounts (e.g. 12% vs 18%), preventing audit demand notices under Form GST DRC-01B.

6. One-Click Audit Workbook

Exports audit-grade multi-sheet Excel workbooks with verified AP hold statuses, ready to be attached to statutory tax audit working papers and engagement files.
